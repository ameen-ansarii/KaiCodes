---
name: Bytewise visual direction
description: Durable visual and product direction for the Bytewise DSA practice app.
---

Bytewise should feel like a friendly daily coding coach: original character-led visuals, bold rounded hierarchy, green/blue primary actions, warm XP/streak accents, and one clear practice action per screen. It can take high-level inspiration from gamified learning products without reproducing their mascot, illustrations, or brand assets.

**Why:** The user requested a Duolingo-inspired experience but the DSA product needs its own identity and assets.

**How to apply:** Preserve the playful reward loop, generous whitespace, chunky pressed controls, and visual progress language when extending the app; keep the Bytewise code-character mark and developer-focused copy distinct.
